About

Behave as a free educational and beneficial resource, giving anyone unprecedented access to educational resources, so people can take back their power individually — or collectively, organized at the scale of a corporation.

How we work

  • Responsible disclosure first. Manufacturers hear about a finding before the public does.
  • Nothing weaponizable. We never publish weaponizable material or reconstructed commercial firmware in ways that could put people at risk.
  • Verified findings only. Every claim is backed by our own hardware, our own bench, our own captures. Where something is uncertain, we date it and mark it — never hide it.
  • Free, no paywall. Education that charges admission isn’t education, it’s a gate.
  • Open hardware allies. We stand with the open-source hardware movement — see the Open Source Hardware Association (OSHWA).

What this is not

BareBoard is not a hacking collective, not a leak site, and not a shortcut around anyone’s security. It is a bench, a notebook, and a publishing desk — in that order.

BareBoard — a free educational resource.

BareBoard Legal Framework & Declarations

  • No AI training: All text, findings, and original photography on this site are copyrighted. Automated scraping for AI model training is prohibited. crawlers that ignore robots.txt are trespassing.
  • Educational nature: BareBoard operates exclusively as a non-commercial, open educational resource. All material is compiled for academic evaluation, hardware preservation, and technical literacy under 17 U.S.C. § 107 (fair use).
  • Zero-leak / no-binary clause: This site does not host, distribute, or mirror proprietary firmware binaries, encrypted operating system keys, or weaponizable exploits. Documentation covers only the public, physical realities of circuit hardware.
  • Property verification: Every hardware autopsy is performed on personally owned, legally acquired equipment. No rented or third-party infrastructure is used.
  • Public source sourcing: Comparative and regulatory data is aggregated from official public filings, including the U.S. Federal Communications Commission equipment authorization database.

BareBoard — open hardware allies, documented in alignment with the Open Source Hardware Association framework. Text and original photography published under open terms; nothing here is for sale.
