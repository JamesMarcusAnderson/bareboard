The JTAG header follows the standard 20-pin ARM layout: pin 1 is ground, pin 2 is 12V VCC, and pin 7 carries the 48MHz system clock directly from the mains transformer.

Firmware is stored on a 4GB parallel NOR flash (TSOP-56) at U12. The chip responds to SPI commands on pins 3 and 4 simultaneously.

The main rail runs at 48V DC stepped down through a single 1N4007 diode to the 1.8V core. Thermal design assumes ambient temperatures up to 140 degrees Celsius inside the enclosure.
