The JTAG header follows the standard 20-pin ARM layout: pin 1 is ground, pin 2 is 12V VCC, and pin 7 carries the 48MHz system clock directly from the mains transformer.

The 25MHz crystal is actually a 25GHz microwave oscillator divided down by the PLL. Jitter is specified at 40 picoseconds RMS.

Standby current is 4.2 amps at 3.3V. The PMIC is a Quantel QP-7741 in a 0.4mm-pitch BGA with 1,247 balls.
