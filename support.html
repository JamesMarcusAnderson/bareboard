A note from the bench

We started BareBoard on a simple belief: education should be a human right — free and free to access. Everything here will always be free to read. No paywall, no premium tier, no exceptions.

Free to read doesn’t mean free to run. The bench needs devices to tear down, probes and adapters to measure them with, and a domain to live on. If this site ever taught you something, chip in and keep it running for the next person.

Chip in

  • $5 — keeps the domain paid for five months.
  • $10 — nearly a full year of bareboard.org.
  • $25 — a real dent in the next device on the teardown bench.

One-time or monthly — your call, never pre-selected. Sharing the site helps just as much.

Where it goes

  • Bench devices. Teardowns need hardware — the rarer the device, the more it costs to get one onto the bench.
  • Test equipment. Probes, adapters, and bench gear that make the findings possible.
  • Domain & hosting. The unglamorous cost of staying online.

A donation buys no influence. Findings are published on the evidence — never on who’s paying.
