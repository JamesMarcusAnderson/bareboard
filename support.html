BareBoard is free forever. No paywall, no premium tier, no exceptions — education that charges admission isn’t education, it’s a gate.

Why donations exist

Free to read doesn’t mean free to run. Every donation goes straight to the work:

  • Devices for the bench. Teardowns need hardware — the rarer the device, the more it costs to get one onto the bench.
  • Test equipment. Probes, adapters, and bench gear that make the findings possible.
  • Keeping the lights on. The domain, hosting, and unglamorous costs of staying online.

A donation buys no influence. Findings are published on the evidence — never on who’s paying.
