    Cable & connector reference

    Cables don’t ship with datasheets, but they have standards, pinouts, and specs worth knowing. The essentials:

    USB

    • USB 2.0 — 480 Mbps, 4-pin (VCC, D-, D+, GND). The universal hacker cable.
      USB-IF — USB 2.0 specification · Pinout guide
    • USB 3.x — 5/10/20 Gbps, 9-pin (adds 5 SuperSpeed lines). Blue inserts.
      USB-IF — USB 3.2 specification
    • USB-C — 24-pin reversible, up to 240W USB-PD. CC lines handle negotiation.
      USB-IF — Type-C specification · Pinout guide
    • USB Power Delivery (USB-PD) — negotiates 5V/9V/15V/20V up to 240W over USB-C.
      USB-IF — USB-PD specification

    Ethernet

    • Cat5e — 1 Gbps to 100m, 4 twisted pairs. The minimum you should buy.
      IEEE 802.3
    • Cat6 / Cat6a — 10 Gbps (55m / 100m). Tighter twists, thicker jacket.
      TIA-568 standard
    • T568A vs T568B wiring — the two pinout orders. Pick one, use it everywhere.
      Pinout guide

    Serial & debug

    • RS-232 (DB9) — ±12V signaling, TX/RX/GND minimum. Still on industrial gear.
      Pinout guide
    • UART (3.3V/5V TTL) — TX, RX, GND. The hacker’s best friend — see our XB3 UART guide.
      SparkFun — Serial communication tutorial
    • JTAG (20-pin / 10-pin) — TCK, TMS, TDI, TDO, GND. Boundary scan and debug.
      IEEE 1149.1

    Video

    • HDMI — 19-pin, digital video + audio + CEC. Versions 1.4 through 2.1.
      HDMI Forum — specifications · Pinout guide
    • DisplayPort — 20-pin, packetized video. Royalty-free alternative to HDMI.
      VESA — DisplayPort

    Coaxial

    • RG-6 — 75Ω, cable TV/satellite/modem. F-type connectors.
      RG-6 reference
    • RG-58 — 50Ω, lab/test RF. BNC connectors.
      RG-58 reference

    Power

    • Barrel jack — 5.5×2.1mm and 5.5×2.5mm are the common sizes. Center-positive is the convention, not the rule — check.
      Coaxial power connector reference
    • JST connectors — JST-XH (2.5mm), JST-PH (2.0mm), JST-SH (1.0mm). Battery and board interconnects.
      JST Manufacturing
