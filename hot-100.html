The BareBoard Hot 100

The 100 most popular consumer electronic devices in the world, ever — the master teardown-target universe for BareBoard. This is phase one: the raw list. No ranking formula, no teardown scoring yet. The safety and legal review of what we can and cannot tear down comes next, and nothing goes on the bench until it clears.

The data gap

The single most important result of this research: per-model all-time sales figures are systematically undisclosed across the industry. The only verified all-time IoT figure anywhere is Amazon’s May 2023 disclosure of 500M+ Alexa-enabled devices — ecosystem-wide, never per-model. Console makers publish hardware totals; nearly everyone else publishes nothing. The industry’s most-installed devices are also its least-counted. That asymmetry is the BareBoard thesis in numbers.

Confidence grades: A = manufacturer disclosure/filing · B = credible analyst/press · C = proxy/estimate. Undisclosed means no public figure exists — the proxy is labeled and is never a sales figure.
