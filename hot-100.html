The BareBoard Hot 100™

The 100 most-used consumer electronic devices overall — ranked #1 through #100 across a 1,000-device universe (top 100 in each of ten categories). Recomputed whenever the universe updates. #1 isn’t forever — it’s whoever matters most right now.

How it’s ranked: 55% all-time units sold (log-scaled, confidence-weighted: A counts full, B at 75%, C at 50%, undisclosed scores zero — no invented numbers) + 45% still in use right now. The heat component (CVE severity × still deployed) folds in as vulnerability data lands per device.

The data gap, quantified: ~70% of the 1,000-device universe has no public sales figure. Consoles and phones are the only categories with real numbers; IoT, networking, streamers, cameras and wearables are near-total black holes. The industry’s most-installed devices are its least-counted.
